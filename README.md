# Kartify

A high-performance business management software and CRM engine designed for client management, order pipeline tracking, dynamic invoicing, and predictive cross-sell analytics.

## Live Prototype Demo

An interactive demo workspace pre-seeded with sample datasets, pipeline cards, and financial records is available online:

https://zmajzla.github.io/Kartify-Demo-SaaS/

To access immediately, click **"Launch Demo Workspace"** on the login screen.

---

## Key Features

### Overview & Financial Analytics
* **KPI Metrics:** Live tracking of gross revenue, active orders, total client base, and stock lines.
* **Extrapolated Trends:** Projected daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and yearly financial metrics calculated from workspace activity timelines.
* **P&L Breakdown:** Real-time visibility into Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), platform processing fees, and net profit margins.

### Client Directory & Intelligence
* **Client Profiles:** Record company names, primary contacts, email addresses, phone numbers, industries, and custom tags.
* **Side-Drawer Intelligence:** Slide-out drawer displaying lifetime value (LTV), order history, and contact details per client.
* **Predictive Recommendations:** Client-side intelligence engine analysing historical data to suggest relevant next-best purchases alongside automated pitch email generation.

### Product Catalogue & Orders
* **Inventory Tracking:** Support for digital items/subscriptions (infinite stock) and physical products with automatic low-stock alerts.
* **Category Auto-Fill:** Form assistant that maps items to categories and sets stock tracking rules based on item names.
* **Multi-Product Orders:** Process orders containing multiple catalogue items, custom quantities, and promotional discount codes.

### Invoicing & Monetisation
* **1.9% Processing Fee Engine:** Built-in fee calculation model for platform monetisation.
* **Digital Invoice Preview:** Render formal invoices featuring client details, itemised breakdowns, discounts, and custom payment links.
* **A4 PDF Export:** Dedicated print stylesheet for exporting standard A4 printable PDF documents.

### System Utilities
* **Command Palette (Cmd + K / Ctrl + K):** Global keyboard shortcut overlay for instant navigation, actions, and client search.
* **Promotional Engine:** Create flat-rate or percentage discount codes (`WELCOME10`, `VIPLOYALTY`) with auto-suggest rules for new or returning clients.
* **CSV Export:** Export directory listings to CSV for external record keeping.
* **Simulated Verification:** Authentication flow featuring simulated OTP (One-Time Password) email verification toasts.
